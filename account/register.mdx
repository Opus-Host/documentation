Creating a Opus Host account is free and takes under two minutes. You just need an email address — no credit card, no commitments. Once you're registered and verified, you'll have access to the dashboard where you can claim your first free VPS.

## What you need

Before you start, make sure you have the following ready:

- **A valid email address** — used for account verification and renewal reminders
- **A username** — 3–20 characters, using only letters, numbers, and underscores
- **A strong password** — at least 8 characters; a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols is recommended

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the registration page">
    Navigate to the Opus Host registration page in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your username">
    Choose a unique username between 3 and 20 characters. Only letters, numbers, and underscores are allowed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Provide a valid email address you have access to. This will be used to verify your account and send you important notices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a strong password">
    Pick a password that is at least 8 characters long. Using a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols makes your account more secure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Account">
    Review your details, then click **Create Account** to submit your registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Opus Host. Click the confirmation link inside to activate your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you don't see the verification email, check your spam or junk folder. The confirmation link expires after **24 hours** — if it expires, you can request a new one from the login page.
</Note>

## After registration

Once you've clicked the confirmation link and verified your email, you can log in to the Opus Host dashboard. From there, you're ready to claim your first free VPS. Head over to [Claim Your VPS](/claim-your-vps) for a step-by-step walkthrough of the process.

## Account security tips

Keeping your account secure protects your server and your data. Here are a few best practices:

- **Use a unique password** — don't reuse a password from another service
- **Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)** if it is available on your account
- **Never share your login credentials** with anyone else, including friends or colleagues

<Warning>
  Sharing your account with others is a violation of Opus Host's Terms of Service and may result in your account being **permanently suspended**. Each person must register their own account.
</Warning>
