## What is a VPS?

A virtual private server (VPS) is your own slice of a physical server hosted in a data center. Unlike shared hosting — where many websites compete for the same resources — a VPS gives you dedicated CPU, RAM, and storage that only you control. You get full root (administrator) access, meaning you can install any software, run any service, and configure the system exactly the way you want. Think of it as a computer in the cloud that's always on and always connected to the internet.

## Use cases

Opus Host is flexible enough to power a wide range of personal and professional projects.

**Hosting websites** — Deploy a personal portfolio, a blog, or a small business site. Install Nginx or Apache, point your domain at your VPS's IP address, and you're live on the internet.

**Running Discord bots** — Keep your Discord bot online 24/7 without leaving your own computer running. Deploy your bot code, start the process, and it will keep running even after you close your laptop.

**Game servers** — Host a private Minecraft, Terraria, or other game server for you and your friends. You control the settings, the mods, and who gets to join.

**Learning Linux** — A VPS is one of the best environments for learning Linux system administration. You have full root access to experiment with, and if something goes wrong you can simply delete and re-provision a fresh server in seconds.
